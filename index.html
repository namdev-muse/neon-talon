NEON TALON

Fly the canyon. Manage your heat. Break the carriers.

3D graphics couldn't start in this browser — Neon Talon needs WebGL. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a device with graphics support.

How to Play

  • Fly the canyon. Steer with the mouse or WASD / arrows. The canyon walls scrape your hull — stay off the rock.
  • Boost with Shift to close the gap — but boost builds heat. At full heat you overheat: boost locks out and your guns fire half as fast until you cool down. Every burst is a decision.
  • Guns fire with Space or by holding the mouse button. Aim by pointing your nose at the target — steering IS aiming.
  • Missiles need a lock: hold a target inside the reticle for a second until it turns gold, then fire with E or right-click. One missile per lock — re-lock to fire again.
  • Drones drift into your lane and ram. Interceptors strafe and shoot. Turrets guard the walls. Carriers are gun-armoured and fill the canyon — you cannot slip past one. Break it with missiles.
  • Reach the end of the canyon alive to clear the sortie. Score comes from kills, time and hull left. Your best result is saved per sortie — beat it.

Controls

  • Mouse / WASD / arrows: steer · Shift: boost · Space / click: fire guns
  • E / right-click: fire missile (needs a lock) · P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Touch: drag to steer · FIRE / BOOST / MISSILE buttons
